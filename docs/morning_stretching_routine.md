# Morning Stretching Routine — Shoulders & Hips
## Weekly Schedule Integrated with Meditation + Core Program

---

## Why These Two Areas

**Hips:** At sub-3:00 marathon pace you're cycling through ~180 strides/minute for nearly 3 hours. Hip flexors shorten from sitting (work) and tighten from running (repetitive sagittal plane). Tight hip flexors pull your pelvis into anterior tilt, which kills glute activation and loads your lower back. Tight hip rotators limit single-leg stability. Everything downstream — knee tracking, foot strike, pelvic drop — traces back to hip mobility.

**Shoulders:** Runners neglect these because "shoulders don't run." But thoracic extension and shoulder mobility directly affect breathing mechanics. Tight pecs and anterior shoulders from desk work pull you into kyphosis, which compresses the ribcage and limits diaphragm excursion. For someone doing 12 min of resonance breathing every morning, restricted thoracic mobility is literally throttling your primary training tool.

---

## Placement in the Morning Stack

```
UPDATED MORNING ORDER:

1. Stretching routine          10-15 min  ← NEW
2. Breathwork                  10-12 min
3. Core rotation               10-15 min
4. Supporting muscle work      0-25 min (easy/rest days only)
5. Run
```

**Stretching goes FIRST**, before breathwork. Two reasons: (1) opening the thoracic spine and shoulders immediately improves breathing quality for the resonance/CO2 session that follows, and (2) hip openers warm up the patterns you'll load during core work and running. The breathwork session becomes a natural transition from passive stretching to active work.

## Stretch Library

### Shoulder & Thoracic Openers

| # | Stretch | Target | Hold / Reps | Cues |
|---|---------|--------|-------------|------|
| S1 | **Thread the needle** | Thoracic rotation, posterior shoulder | 30s each side | On all fours, slide one arm under the other, let the shoulder drop to the floor. Breathe into the stretch — the rib expansion IS the stretch. |
| S2 | **Open book** | Thoracic rotation, pec, anterior shoulder | 30s each side | Side-lying, knees stacked at 90°. Top arm opens like a book toward the opposite side. Follow the hand with your eyes. Don't force the shoulder down — let gravity work. |
| S3 | **Wall slides (CARs)** | Shoulder flexion, upward rotation, serratus | 8-10 reps | Back flat against wall, arms in "goal post." Slide arms overhead maintaining wall contact with hands, elbows, and low back. If low back lifts off, you've gone too far. |
| S4 | **Doorway pec stretch** | Pec major/minor, anterior deltoid | 30s each side | Forearm on doorframe at 90°. Step through. Adjust elbow height: low = upper pec, high = lower pec. Do both positions. |
| S5 | **Cat-cow** | Thoracic flexion/extension, spinal segmentation | 10 slow reps | Initiate from the pelvis, not the neck. Let the movement wave up the spine. Exhale fully into cat (maximizes thoracic flexion). |
| S6 | **Prone Y-T-W raises** | Lower trapezius, rotator cuff, scapular stability | 8 reps each position | Lying face down, arms form Y, then T, then W. Lift hands 2 inches off floor, hold 3s. Thumbs up. This is activation, not a stretch — but it's what cements the mobility you just opened. |
| S7 | **Cross-body shoulder stretch** | Posterior deltoid, infraspinatus | 30s each side | Pull one arm across your chest with the opposite hand. Keep the shoulder down — don't let it hike up toward your ear. |
| S8 | **Supine thoracic extension on foam roller** | Thoracic extension, rib cage expansion | 60s | Lie lengthwise on foam roller (spine along the roller). Arms fall open to the sides, palms up. Breathe deeply — each exhale lets the shoulders drop further. This is the single best position for opening breathing mechanics. |

### Hip Openers

| # | Stretch | Target | Hold / Reps | Cues |
|---|---------|--------|-------------|------|
| H1 | **90/90 hip switches** | Internal/external rotation, deep rotators | 10 reps (5 each direction) | Sit with both knees at 90°. Rotate from one side to the other. Keep spine tall — don't lean to compensate. This is mobility AND motor control. |
| H2 | **Pigeon pose (or figure-4 supine)** | Piriformis, external rotators, glute | 45s each side | If pigeon is too intense on the knee, do figure-4 lying on your back. Breathe into the deep hip. Don't force depth — consistency over intensity. |
| H3 | **Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch** | Psoas, rectus femoris, anterior hip capsule | 45s each side | Rear knee on pad, squeeze the rear glute to posteriorly tilt the pelvis BEFORE leaning forward. Most people skip the glute squeeze and just lean, which arches the low back instead of stretching the hip flexor. |
| H4 | **Frog stretch** | Adductors, medial hip capsule | 45s | On all fours, widen knees as far as comfortable, feet turned out. Rock gently forward and back. Don't bounce. Progress width over weeks. |
| H5 | **Supine spinal twist** | Hip rotators, thoracolumbar fascia, obliques | 30s each side | Lying on back, one knee across the body. Opposite shoulder stays on the floor. Let gravity do the work. This connects hip mobility to thoracic rotation — the exact chain used in running. |
| H6 | **Hip CARs (controlled articular rotations)** | Full hip ROM, capsular health | 5 each direction, each side | Standing on one leg, draw the biggest circle possible with the free knee. Slow and controlled. This maps out your available range and lubricates the joint. |
| H7 | **Couch stretch** | Rectus femoris, psoas (deep stretch) | 45s each side | Rear foot on a couch or wall behind you, front foot in lunge. Squeeze rear glute. This is the most aggressive hip flexor stretch — use on days when H3 feels too easy. |
| H8 | **Adductor rockback** | Adductors, medial hamstring | 30s each side | Half-kneeling, extend one leg straight to the side. Rock your hips back toward the heel of the kneeling leg. Keeps the spine neutral. |

## Weekly Rotation (3 Days, Alternating Focus)

Stretching is done 7 days a week, but the program rotates through 3 templates. This gives enough variety to prevent boredom while ensuring every pattern gets hit twice per week minimum.

### Template 1 — Thoracic Priority
*Best before breathwork sessions where breathing quality matters most (Resonance Breathing, CO2 Tolerance)*

| Order | Exercise | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Cat-cow (S5) | 10 reps (~90s) |
| 2 | Foam roller thoracic extension (S8) | 60s |
| 3 | Thread the needle (S1) | 30s each side |
| 4 | Open book (S2) | 30s each side |
| 5 | Doorway pec stretch (S4) | 30s each side |
| 6 | Half-kneeling hip flexor stretch (H3) | 45s each side |
| 7 | 90/90 hip switches (H1) | 10 reps (~90s) |
| 8 | Supine spinal twist (H5) | 30s each side |

**Total: ~12 min.** Heavy on thoracic/shoulder, lighter hip maintenance.

### Template 2 — Hip Priority
*Best on easy/rest days when you have more time and aren't running hard afterward*

| Order | Exercise | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Hip CARs (H6) | 5 each direction, each side (~3 min) |
| 2 | 90/90 hip switches (H1) | 10 reps (~90s) |
| 3 | Pigeon or figure-4 (H2) | 45s each side |
| 4 | Frog stretch (H4) | 45s |
| 5 | Couch stretch (H7) | 45s each side |
| 6 | Adductor rockback (H8) | 30s each side |
| 7 | Cat-cow (S5) | 8 reps (~60s) |
| 8 | Thread the needle (S1) | 30s each side |

**Total: ~14 min.** Deep hip work, thoracic maintenance.

### Template 3 — Integrated / Dynamic
*Best on hard training days — shorter, more movement-based, functions as warm-up*

| Order | Exercise | Duration |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Cat-cow (S5) | 8 reps (~60s) |
| 2 | Hip CARs (H6) | 3 each direction, each side (~2 min) |
| 3 | 90/90 hip switches (H1) | 8 reps (~60s) |
| 4 | Half-kneeling hip flexor (H3) | 30s each side |
| 5 | Open book (S2) | 30s each side |
| 6 | Wall slides (S3) | 8 reps (~60s) |
| 7 | Prone Y-T-W (S6) | 6 reps each (~90s) |
| 8 | Supine spinal twist (H5) | 20s each side |

**Total: ~10 min.** Balanced, quicker, activation-oriented.

## Weekly Assignment

| Day | Training Type | Stretch Template | Rationale |
|-----|--------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Mon | Hard (intervals) | **Template 3** — Integrated/Dynamic | Short, warm-up oriented before hard effort |
| Tue | Easy | **Template 2** — Hip Priority | Deep hip work, have time and bandwidth |
| Wed | Hard (tempo) | **Template 3** — Integrated/Dynamic | Short, warm-up oriented |
| Thu | Easy | **Template 1** — Thoracic Priority | Open breathing mechanics, alternate from Tue's hip focus |
| Fri | Rest | **Template 2** — Hip Priority | Biggest mobility session of the week, no run to rush to |
| Sat | Hard (long run) | **Template 1** — Thoracic Priority | Open rib cage for 2+ hour breathing, some hip maintenance |
| Sun | Easy | **Template 3** — Integrated/Dynamic | Balanced light session, recovery day after long run |

**Weekly coverage:** Each template hits 2-3x/week. Hips get dedicated deep work 2x (Tue, Fri). Thoracic gets priority 2x (Thu, Sat). Every day includes at least some of both.

## Progression

### Weeks 1-4: Establish Range
- Use prescribed hold times
- Don't push into pain — find the "edge" and breathe there
- Log which stretches feel most restricted (this tells you where to invest more time later)

### Weeks 5-8: Extend Holds on Tight Areas
- Increase holds to 60s on your 2-3 tightest stretches
- Add a second set on your most restricted hip pattern (usually hip flexor or piriformis for runners)
- Start doing foam roller thoracic extension (S8) daily regardless of template

### Weeks 9+: Add Loaded Mobility
- Half-kneeling hip flexor: hold a light KB overhead on the stretching side (adds lateral flexion and deeper psoas stretch)
- 90/90: add forward lean to increase intensity on lead-leg external rotators
- Wall slides: add light resistance band for scapular loading
- Pigeon: progress to active pigeon (lift and lower the torso slowly)

## Travel Adaptation (Costa Rica / Panama)

Every stretch in this program is bodyweight and floor-only — no equipment needed. The full routine travels perfectly. If floor space is limited (hotel room), prioritize:

1. Cat-cow (S5)
2. Half-kneeling hip flexor (H3)
3. 90/90 switches (H1)
4. Open book (S2)
5. Supine spinal twist (H5)

That's ~8 min and covers the essentials of both shoulder and hip mobility.

**Drop the foam roller work while traveling** unless your accommodation has one. Don't buy one for a 3-week trip — a rolled-up towel works for a crude substitute for thoracic extension.
