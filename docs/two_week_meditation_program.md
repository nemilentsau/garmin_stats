# Two-Week Meditation & Breathwork Program
## Day-by-Day Specification | Phase 1 Foundation

---

## Overview

A 14-day introductory program for systematic development of autonomic control and attentional skills. Periodized around marathon training load. Designed for HRV-tested experimentation using Garmin Epix Pro Gen 2 morning rMSSD.

**Core Philosophy:** No fake metrics, no gamification. Pure training tool with experimental rigor.

**Prerequisites:** None. Program assumes zero meditation/breathwork experience and builds from minimal doses to the full 3x/day protocol.

---

## Protocol Reference

| Protocol | Pattern | Primary Target |
|----------|---------|----------------|
| Resonance Breathing | 5s in / 5s out (~6 bpm) | Vagal tone, baroreflex training |
| Extended Exhale | 4s in / 7s out | Parasympathetic activation, recovery |
| Box Breathing | 4s in / 4s hold / 4s out / 4s hold | Arousal control, composure |
| CO2 Tolerance | 4s in / 6s out / 4s post-exhale hold | CO2 comfort threshold |
| Focused Attention | Breath counting or single-point focus | Concentration stability |
| Open Monitoring | Non-directive awareness, no anchor | Interoceptive clarity, non-reactivity |

---

## Week 1: Establish the Habit

**Goal:** Build consistency at 2x/day (morning + evening). Introduce all core protocols. Collect HRV baseline. Learn the post-session rating system.

**Training week assumed:** Mon intervals, Tue easy, Wed tempo, Thu easy, Fri rest, Sat long run, Sun easy.

---

### Day 1 — Monday (Hard: Intervals)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 8 min | Shorter than target. Focus on finding the 5s/5s rhythm. Don't worry about depth — just get the timing right. Do this before your Garmin HRV reading. |
| Midday | — | — | No midday session in Week 1. |
| Evening | Extended Exhale | 6 min | First exposure. 4s in / 7s out may feel awkward — the long exhale is the point. Do this 30-60 min before bed. |

**Post-session logging:** After each session, rate attention stability (1-5), relaxation depth (1-5), mental clarity after (1-5). First day ratings are your personal baseline — don't aim for high numbers, just record honestly.

**HRV note:** Record morning HRV. This is Day 1 of your baseline period.

---

### Day 2 — Tuesday (Easy Run)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 8 min | Same as yesterday. Building pattern recognition — your body learns the rhythm through repetition, not intensity. |
| Midday | — | — | No midday session. |
| Evening | Focused Attention | 10 min | First meditation session. Breath counting: count each exhale from 1 to 10, restart at 1. When you lose count (you will), just restart. The restart IS the training — noticing distraction is the skill. |

**Why Focused Attention tonight instead of Extended Exhale:** Easy day = bandwidth for skill development. Hard days get breathwork (autonomic recovery). Easy days get meditation (attentional training). This alternation is the core periodization logic.

---

### Day 3 — Wednesday (Hard: Tempo)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 10 min | Increase by 2 min. If 5s/5s feels natural, stay there. If it feels forced, try 4.5s/4.5s and work up. |
| Midday | — | — | No midday session. |
| Evening | Extended Exhale | 8 min | Increase by 2 min. Hard training day = breathwork for recovery. Focus on making the exhale smooth and complete — don't rush the last second. |

---

### Day 4 — Thursday (Easy Run)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 10 min | Same duration as yesterday. Consistency before progression. |
| Midday | — | — | No midday session. |
| Evening | Open Monitoring | 10 min | First exposure to Open Monitoring. No breath counting — just sit and notice whatever arises (sounds, body sensations, thoughts) without following any of it. This feels harder than Focused Attention because there's no anchor. That's normal. |

**Contrast with Day 2:** Focused Attention = narrow beam (one object). Open Monitoring = wide lens (everything). Both are skills. You need both for running — concentration for pacing discipline, open awareness for reading your body.

---

### Day 5 — Friday (Rest Day)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Box Breathing | 6 min | First exposure. 4-4-4-4 pattern. The holds are new — they create a different autonomic signature than Resonance or Extended Exhale. Focus on making the transitions smooth (inhale→hold→exhale→hold) without gasping. |
| Midday | — | — | No midday session. |
| Evening | Focused Attention | 12 min | Longest session so far. Rest day = more bandwidth = push duration. Same breath counting. Track how many complete 1-10 cycles you manage — this number will improve over weeks. |

**Why Box Breathing today:** Rest day is when you introduce new protocols. Box Breathing on a fresh nervous system is easier to learn than after a hard workout. You'll use this pre-race and pre-interview — today is building familiarity.

---

### Day 6 — Saturday (Hard: Long Run)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 10 min | Standard morning priming before the long run. This is your pre-long-run ritual from now on. |
| Midday | — | — | No midday session. |
| Evening | Extended Exhale | 10 min | Full target duration. After a long run, this is the highest-value session of the week. Your body is in a recovery deficit — parasympathetic activation here directly supports overnight repair. Non-negotiable. |

**HRV note:** Tomorrow morning's HRV is the first interesting data point: long run day + evening Extended Exhale → what happens to next-morning rMSSD?

---

### Day 7 — Sunday (Easy Run)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 10 min | Check in: does 5s/5s feel natural now? If yes, you're ready for Week 2 progressions. If still awkward, stay at this duration. |
| Midday | — | — | No midday session. |
| Evening | Open Monitoring | 12 min | Second exposure, slightly longer. Notice if your mind is quieter after a week of practice vs. Day 4. Don't judge — just notice. |

## Week 2: Add the Midday Slot

**Goal:** Scale to 3x/day. Introduce CO2 Tolerance. Increase durations toward full target. Sharpen the periodization logic (hard day vs. easy day protocol selection becomes automatic).

---

### Day 8 — Monday (Hard: Intervals)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 12 min | Full target duration. You've earned the increase through a week of consistency. |
| Midday | Box Breathing | 5 min | **First midday session.** Keep it short. The point is establishing the third slot, not loading it. Do this during a work break at a consistent time. |
| Evening | Extended Exhale | 10 min | Hard day = recovery breathwork. Standard. |

**Midday introduction logic:** The midday slot starts small (5 min breathwork) on a hard day because you want the lowest possible friction for building the new habit. Don't start the third slot with a 10 min meditation that requires concentration — that's asking too much on day one of the new rhythm.

---

### Day 9 — Tuesday (Easy Run)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 10 min | Easy day = shorter morning breathwork (maintenance dose). |
| Midday | Focused Attention | 10 min | First midday meditation. Same breath counting as evening sessions. Log attention_stability carefully — midday ratings are your cognitive state measure. |
| Evening | Open Monitoring | 15 min | Full target duration. Easy day = meditation evening. The jump from 12 to 15 min is manageable after a week of practice. |

**This is the first full easy-day template:** Resonance → Focused Attention → Open Monitoring. If this feels like "a lot of sitting," that's correct. Easy days are where the attentional skills develop. Breathwork alone doesn't build concentration or interoceptive clarity.

---

### Day 10 — Wednesday (Hard: Tempo)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 12 min | Full duration. Hard day priming. |
| Midday | Box Breathing | 5 min | Same as Day 8. Hard day midday stays short and breathwork-based. Optional — if the tempo session wiped you out, skip without guilt. |
| Evening | Extended Exhale | 10 min | Recovery. Standard. |

---

### Day 11 — Thursday (Easy Run)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 10 min | Maintenance dose. |
| Midday | Focused Attention | 10 min | Second midday meditation. Compare your attention_stability rating to Day 9 — same or higher? You're on track. |
| Evening | Open Monitoring | 15 min | Full duration. Practice non-reactive observation: sounds arise, you notice them, they pass. Thoughts arise, same thing. The skill is in the "not following." |

**Attentional training note:** By now you should notice a difference between Focused Attention (effortful, narrow, tiring) and Open Monitoring (receptive, wide, subtler). Focused Attention builds the muscle. Open Monitoring builds the flexibility. Runners need both — the ability to lock onto pace AND the ability to read the body's signals without panicking.

---

### Day 12 — Friday (Rest Day)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | CO2 Tolerance | 10 min | **First exposure to the final protocol.** 4s in / 6s out / 4s post-exhale hold. The post-exhale hold is new and will feel uncomfortable — that's the training stimulus. Your body wants to inhale; you're teaching it to tolerate the CO2 buildup. Start with 3s holds if 4s is too much. |
| Midday | Focused Attention | 12 min | Longest midday sit. Rest day = push the edges. You have no run to recover from and no run to prepare for. |
| Evening | Open Monitoring | 15 min | OR Extended Exhale 8 min if you feel activated/restless. This is the only conditional session — use subjective arousal as the selector. If you can sit still comfortably, Open Monitoring. If your mind is racing, Extended Exhale brings you down. |

**CO2 Tolerance introduction logic:** Same as Box Breathing last week — new protocols debut on rest days when the nervous system is fresh. CO2 Tolerance is the most physiologically demanding breathwork protocol. Don't introduce it on a training day.

---

### Day 13 — Saturday (Hard: Long Run)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 12 min | Full duration pre-long-run. This is now your established ritual. |
| Midday | Box Breathing | 5 min | Optional. Post-long-run you may be too tired for anything. If you do it, treat it as a recovery tool, not training. |
| Evening | Extended Exhale | 10 min | Non-negotiable after a long run. This is the session with the strongest theoretical link to next-morning HRV recovery. Compare tomorrow's HRV to last Sunday's (Day 7) — you now have a week-over-week comparison with the same training stimulus. |

---

### Day 14 — Sunday (Easy Run)

| Slot | Protocol | Duration | Notes |
|------|----------|----------|-------|
| Morning | Resonance Breathing | 10 min | Maintenance dose. |
| Midday | Focused Attention | 10 min | Compare attention_stability to your Day 2 evening Focused Attention session (your first ever). Has the number moved? |
| Evening | Open Monitoring | 15 min | Last session of the two-week program. Sit with no agenda. Notice what's changed since Day 4 (your first Open Monitoring). Don't analyze during the session — just sit. Analyze after. |

### HRV Data to Review at Day 14

By end of Day 14 you have 14 morning HRV readings. Look for:

1. **Week 2 average vs Week 1 average.** Any upward shift? (Don't expect much in 2 weeks — you're looking for direction, not magnitude.)
2. **Post-long-run recovery.** Compare Day 7 morning HRV (after Day 6 long run + Extended Exhale) to Day 14 morning HRV (after Day 13 long run + Extended Exhale). Same stimulus, one more week of training — is recovery faster?
3. **Hard day vs easy day.** Is your HRV consistently lower after hard training days? (It should be. This confirms your measurement is sensitive enough to detect training load.)
4. **Extended Exhale effect.** Compare mornings after Extended Exhale evenings vs mornings after meditation evenings. Any difference? (This is the primary hypothesis to track going forward.)

## What Comes After Day 14

If compliance was >80% and the 3x/day rhythm feels sustainable:

**Weeks 3-4 (full Phase 1 Foundation):**
- Lock in all durations at full targets
- CO2 Tolerance moves to every rest day
- Midday sessions solidify: Box Breathing on hard days, Focused Attention on easy/rest days
- Begin tracking correlations with statistical intent (not just eyeballing HRV)
- Target: >80% compliance sustained

**Weeks 5-8 (Phase 2 Extension):**
- Increase Focused Attention to 15 min on easy days
- Extend Extended Exhale to 12 min if HRV correlation is positive
- Add body scanning to Open Monitoring
- Personalize resonance frequency (test 5.5s, 6s, 6.5s cycles)
- Target: identify top 2 correlations with statistical significance

**Weeks 9+ (Phase 3 Optimization):**
- Double down on protocols with strongest HRV signal
- Cut or reduce protocols showing no effect
- Extend Open Monitoring to 20 min if attention stability ratings consistently >4/5

### Session Timer Specifications

**Resonance Breathing:**
- Visual: expanding/contracting circle synced to 5s/5s
- Audio: optional soft tone at inhale/exhale transitions
- Display: cycle count, elapsed time, remaining time

**Extended Exhale:**
- Visual: asymmetric animation (shorter inhale arc, longer exhale arc)
- Audio: optional tone at transitions
- Exhale phase should feel visually "slower" than inhale

**Box Breathing:**
- Visual: square that traces each side (inhale=left, hold=top, exhale=right, hold=bottom)
- Audio: tone at each corner transition
- Display: current phase label ("inhale", "hold", "exhale", "hold")

**CO2 Tolerance:**
- Visual: similar to Extended Exhale but with a distinct "hold" phase after exhale
- The post-exhale hold should show a countdown
- Color shift during hold (subtle, e.g., blue → slightly warmer) to acknowledge discomfort without alarming

**Focused Attention:**
- Simple countdown timer
- Optional interval bell (e.g., at 5 min mark)
- No distracting UI — blank screen or minimal display

**Open Monitoring:**
- Same as Focused Attention but NO interval bell
- Minimal display — ideally just a subtle "session active" indicator
- End bell only

**All sessions:**
- Gentle start tone
- Clear end tone (different from start)
- Screen stays dim/dark during session
- No notifications pass through during active session
- Post-session rating prompt appears immediately after end tone
