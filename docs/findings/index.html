What these findings say

The strongest claims are not isolated facts. They form a compact story about what this dataset can and cannot support.

  • The useful structure is a recovery/stress axis. HRV, resting heart rate, stress, and body battery move together strongly enough to justify a composite recovery view; SpO2, skin temperature, REM, and deep sleep mostly act as separate flags or weak context.
  • The recovery score is a tested product decision. Baseline choice, weighting, smoothing, missing-data rules, and validation checks were compared against the year rather than chosen by intuition.
  • HRV is readable as trajectory, not status. The signal is continuous, volatile night to night, regime-sensitive, and weekly patterned; categorical same-night labels overstate what one night can tell you.

How to read this

Every claim carries a confidence tier. The tier governs how far a finding is allowed to travel — from a one-off curiosity to an asserted decision the product is built on.

  • confident Asserted takeaway. Clears every quality gate with no standing interpretive ceiling, and survived an adversarial self-review. These drive score, axis, flag, and copy decisions.
  • provisional Attributed observation. Multiple methods converge or a pre-registered test passed, but a real ceiling remains (a confound, a shared derivation, a single in-sample event). Carried with its caveats.
  • tentative Exploratory only. A single look, often from a many-comparison sweep. Seeds a question; never asserted.
